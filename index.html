About the channel

How Superintelligence Works explains what today's AI systems can and cannot do, what AGI and superintelligence mean, and what researchers actually forecast. We work from primary sources such as research papers, benchmark leaderboards, expert surveys and official reports, and we say when a number is an estimate, a snapshot or a company's own claim.

Long-form videos are published on YouTube. Short clips from those videos appear as YouTube Shorts and on TikTok, Instagram and X.

How we verify numbers

  1. Every number has a source. Each sentence in a script that contains a number is tagged with its source, and the source list with links goes into the video description and onto the video's sources page.
  2. Fact check before upload. Scripts, on-screen text, titles and descriptions are checked against the sources before anything is uploaded, for long videos and Shorts alike.
  3. Dated snapshots. Benchmark scores and leaderboards change, so we label them with the date of the source ("as of" a month), and note when sources disagree with each other.
  4. Public corrections. If we get something wrong, the correction is logged on that video's sources page.
